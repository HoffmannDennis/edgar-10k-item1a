PART I

Item 1. Business

Example Widget Corp. designs and sells industrial widgets to manufacturers in North America and Europe through direct sales and a network of independent distributors.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Investing in our common stock involves risks. You should carefully consider the risks described below together with the other information in this report. Any of these risks could materially and adversely affect our business, financial condition and results of operations.

Risks Related to Our Business

We depend on a small number of customers for a significant portion of our revenue. Our three largest customers accounted for a substantial share of net sales in the last fiscal year. The loss of any of these customers, or a significant reduction in their orders, could materially reduce our revenue and harm our results of operations.

Increases in the cost of raw materials could reduce our margins. We purchase steel, aluminum and electronic components from a limited number of suppliers. Price increases or supply interruptions may adversely affect our production schedules, and we may be unable to pass higher costs on to our customers.

Competition could reduce demand for our products. We compete with larger companies that have greater financial, technical and marketing resources. Competitive pressure could force us to lower prices, increase spending or lose market share, any of which could harm our financial condition.

Risks Related to Information Technology

A cybersecurity incident could disrupt our operations. We rely on information systems to manage orders, production and payments. A breach, ransomware attack or system failure could interrupt operations, expose confidential information and result in liability, regulatory scrutiny and reputational harm.

Risks Related to Our Common Stock

The market price of our common stock may be volatile. Our stock price may fluctuate significantly because of quarterly results, changes in analyst estimates or general market conditions, and investors could lose part or all of their investment.

Item 1B. Unresolved Staff Comments

None.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

We maintain a risk-based program to assess, identify and manage cybersecurity threats.
